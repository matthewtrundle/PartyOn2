# Local backlink outreach plan — June 2026

**Author:** seo-director.
**Source data:** Ahrefs MCP (free-tier public DR endpoint), GSC raw 2026-06.
**Goal:** Narrow the Domain Rating (DR) gap between partyondelivery.com (DR 12) and the dominant Austin-context competitors (DR 68–90). This is the slowest of the five operator-side workstreams but compounds most over time, especially after the May 2026 Core Update.

---

## Why this matters now

| Domain | DR | What they are |
|---|---:|---|
| **partyondelivery.com** | **12** | Us. |
| do512.com | 70 | Do512 — Austin events / "what's happening" guide |
| austin360.com | 73 | Austin American-Statesman events/lifestyle vertical |
| drizly.com | 76 | National alcohol delivery aggregator (Uber Eats subsidiary) |
| totalwine.com | 78 | National retailer |
| specsonline.com | 68 | Texas-regional alcohol retailer |
| austin.eater.com | 90 | Eater Austin — Vox Media food/drink vertical |

**DR 12 vs 68–90 means competitors have roughly 100–1000× more referring domains than we do.** That's the single biggest reason on-page fixes alone won't fully close the gap — Google's algorithm reads backlinks as "third parties vouching for this site," and we have very few vouchers.

The May 2026 Core Update made this more painful: core updates re-weight content quality, but **authority signals (links) act as a counterweight that lets thin pages still rank if the site is trusted.** Drizly's thin product pages rank because drizly.com is trusted; our equivalent pages don't, partly because partyondelivery.com isn't yet.

**The good news:** at DR 12, *any* link from a DR 40+ site moves the needle. Competitors at DR 76 need DR 80+ links to gain ground — much harder.

---

## Realistic targets and timeline

| Time horizon | DR target | Realistic monthly link gain | Cumulative DR move |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| 3 months | 18–20 | 4–6 quality links/month | +6 to +8 |
| 6 months | 22–26 | 4–6 quality links/month | +10 to +14 |
| 12 months | 30+ | 4–6 quality links/month | +18 to +22 |

DR is non-linear: moving from 12→25 is significantly easier than 25→40. The first year is the biggest payoff window.

**Don't chase volume — chase relevance.** A single link from Eater Austin (DR 90, Austin context, food/drink topical) is worth roughly 30 links from generic directory sites. For a local Austin-only business, ~50 well-chosen Austin/wedding/events links over 12 months will move DR more than 500 generic ones.

## Targets by tier and acquisition strategy

### Tier 1 — Austin local media and event guides (highest leverage)

These are DR 65+ Austin-context sites where one editorial mention can move the needle.

| Site | DR (est.) | Why we want it | Acquisition angle |
|---|---|---|---|
| **austin.eater.com** | 90 | Eater is the most-cited food/drink authority for Austin | Pitch a roundup story: "Austin's Best Boat-Friendly Drink Coolers," "Where to Order Alcohol Delivery in Austin (2026)" |
| **do512.com** | 70 | Events calendar + curated lists — Austin readers | Submit partner-of for Do512 Lounge events; pitch a column on event drink planning |
| **austin360.com** | 73 | Austin American-Statesman lifestyle vertical | PR pitch for seasonal stories (boat season, holiday parties, wedding season) |
| **kut.org** | check DR | Austin NPR | Sponsor a segment or pitch a culture piece about Austin's party logistics |
| **austinchronicle.com** | check DR | Alt-weekly with strong local SEO | Best-of-Austin nominations; sponsored content (transparent) |
| **culturemap.com/austin** | check DR | Lifestyle/events for Austin elites | Pitch luxury-event coverage tied to weddings |
| **365thingsaustin.com** | check DR | Local listicle aggregator | Easy "365 things to do in Austin" features; usually quick yes |
| **austinhomeshow.com** | low | — | Sponsorship / event partnerships, not link-focused |

**Action:** Pitch 1–2 stories per quarter to each of the top 3 (Eater, Do512, Austin360). Yield rate: ~10–20%. If even one lands per quarter, that's 4 high-DR links/year — already a meaningful move.

### Tier 2 — Wedding industry directories and vendor lists

Austin has a robust wedding-vendor ecosystem. Our wedding cluster is gaining ground per GSC (+523 impressions in 60d) — these links compound that.

| Site | Strategy |
|---|---|
| **theknot.com** vendor listing | Free listing with paid upgrade; verify our claim |
| **weddingwire.com** vendor listing | Same; cross-listed with The Knot (XO Group) |
| **zola.com** vendor list | Newer but growing rapidly with millennials |
| **junebugweddings.com** | Editorial focus on real weddings; pitch a "real wedding" feature |
| **stylemepretty.com** | High DR (75+) wedding editorial | Submit real weddings we delivered alcohol for |
| **martha stewart weddings** | High DR but national focus | Long shot — pitch only if exceptional story |
| **Austin-specific wedding blogs** | (e.g. austinweddingvenuesguide.com) | Lower DR but high topical relevance |
| **TX wedding planner Instagram-to-blog links** | Outreach to 10–20 local planners; offer "preferred vendor" deals in exchange for blog mentions |

**Action:** Operator should claim/verify all directory profiles (1 day of work). Junebug + StyleMePretty pitches each take ~2 hr to prepare; do one per month.

### Tier 3 — Wedding/event vendor partnerships (reciprocal/affiliate)

We already have an affiliate program. Each affiliate partner is a potential backlink. Inventory:

| Partner type | Current count (estimate) | Backlink opportunity |
|---|---|---|
| Wedding photographers | Unknown | High — they have blogs |
| DJ services | 1 listed (partners/austin-wedding-dj) | Reciprocal links from DJ vendor pages |
| Caterers | 0 | Pitch reciprocal listing |
| Wedding planners | 0 | Pitch reciprocal listing |
| Boat charter operators (Lake Travis) | Premiere Party Cruise (per memory) | Already have relationship — request a link on their site if not done |
| Venues | 0 | Hill Country venues with blogs — outreach |

**Action:** Sweep `/affiliate` partner list. For each existing partner, audit whether they link to us. If not, send a friendly "would you mind adding our logo to your Vendors page?" — yield rate ~60% for existing partners. This is the highest-success-rate, lowest-effort outreach we can do.

### Tier 4 — Lifestyle / "things to do" content sites

| Site | Strategy |
|---|---|
| **redfin.com/blog** (Austin neighborhood guides) | They commission writers for "best of X" content; offer expert quotes |
| **apartmenttherapy.com** (Austin neighborhoods) | Same — offer expert quotes on hosting |
| **bachelorlive.com** / bach party planning blogs | Submit "Austin guide" articles |
| **bridesmaidforhire** style services | Pitch reciprocal mention |
| **Reddit r/Austin** | NOT a backlink (nofollow) but drives referral traffic; participate honestly, don't spam |

### Tier 5 — Liquor industry / B2B

| Site | Strategy |
|---|---|
| **TABC.texas.gov licensee directory** | We should be listed if we have a TABC license (per memory we do) — verify |
| **Distributor sites** (RNDC, Silver Eagle, etc.) | Some distributor sites link to their retailers — verify |
| **Brewery / winery partner pages** | If we feature a local Austin brewery (Pinthouse, Live Oak, Austin Beerworks), reach out — many breweries link to retailers who carry them |

**Action:** Make a list of every Austin-local brewery/winery/distillery in our catalog. Reach out asking to be listed on their "Where to Buy" page. High yield rate (~70%+) because it's a win for them too.

### Tier 6 — Avoid

| Type | Why avoid |
|---|---|
| Generic "free SEO directory" sites (DR 5–20, no editorial) | Low value, can trigger spam signals |
| Paid link networks / PBNs | Manual penalty risk |
| Comment-link spam, forum profile links | Diluted, easy to identify as low-quality |
| Reciprocal "link exchange" with unrelated sites | Modern Google ignores or penalizes |

## Tracking system

A simple Google Sheet or Notion database works. Required columns:

| Column | Type | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Target URL | text | The specific page we want a link on |
| Target site | text | Just the domain |
| Tier | 1–5 | Strategy bucket |
| DR (Ahrefs) | number | Updated quarterly |
| Contact name | text | |
| Contact email | text | |
| Pitch date | date | |
| Follow-up date | date | Always one — emails get lost |
| Response (Y/N/Pending) | enum | |
| Link landed? | Y/N | The actual outcome |
| Link URL (if landed) | text | For tracking and updating GSC referring domains |
| Notes | text | Pitch angle, what worked, what to retry |

**Cadence:** Operator (or marketing hire) should aim for 5 fresh outreaches/week + 5 follow-ups/week = ~10 emails/week. At ~15% yield, that's 6 links/month. Drop-off after the first follow-up is steep — don't follow up 3+ times unless they've engaged.

## Open questions

1. **Who owns this work?** Backlink outreach can be operator + part-time virtual assistant ($15–25/hr). 10 hours/week of VA time pays for itself within 6 months. Worth deciding before sending Template B/C/D in bulk.
2. **Do we have a press kit?** Even a simple PDF with: company overview, founder bio, headshots, logo files, key data points, recent press hits. Cuts editorial pitch friction by ~70%. If we don't have one, that's a 2-hour build.
3. **Affiliate cookie window vs. backlink credit:** Some affiliate partners will be hesitant to link to us if they think the link will "leak" affiliate sales. We should be clear it doesn't — backlinks for SEO are separate from affiliate tracking.
