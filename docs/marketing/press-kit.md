## Two-paragraph company description (for sponsorships/partnerships)

Party On Delivery is Austin's premium alcohol-and-event-supplies delivery service. Founded in [year] by Allan Henslee and Brian Hill, the company fills the gap left when Drizly wound down: a locally-owned, TABC-licensed operator with its own delivery fleet, direct distributor relationships, and a focus on the specific logistics of Austin events — boat parties, Hill Country weddings, corporate offsites, and group celebrations. We deliver beer, wine, liquor, kegs, mixers, ice, cups, and decor — cold, on time, and with optional bartender service — across the entire Austin metro area.

Operating data tells the story of how Austin parties have shifted: we see ~30 Lake Travis boat weekends per season, increasing demand for low-sugar canned cocktails, and 18 months of detailed order data showing the hour-by-hour rhythm of Austin celebrations. Our group-ordering platform with built-in split-pay was the first in the Austin market and now handles the majority of our wedding and bachelor-party orders. Beyond delivery, we operate an affiliate program with local Austin partners — DJs, photographers, planners, boat charter operators — and we're the preferred bar service vendor for Premiere Party Cruise on Lake Travis.

## Quick facts editors get wrong

A pre-empted clarification list — saves both you and the journalist time.

- **We are not affiliated with Drizly or Uber.** Drizly wound down Austin operations in 2025; we're a separate locally-owned operator.
- **We are TABC-licensed.** All delivery drivers are ID-verified. We do not deliver to minors and check ID on every drop.
- **We do not have a brick-and-mortar storefront.** The address is operations only; we are delivery-only.
- **We deliver to addresses, not events.** Customers schedule delivery to a venue; we do not pour drinks at events unless bartender service is added on.
