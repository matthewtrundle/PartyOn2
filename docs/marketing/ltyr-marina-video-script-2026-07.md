## SHOOT NOTES

- **Shoot vertical.** This lives on Reels/TikTok and inside their booking emails, not on a TV.
- **Wind is the enemy.** Lav mic or wired earbuds mic under the collar. Phone mic on a dock at 2pm is unusable — if the audio is blown you can re-record VO later, but only if you shoot the walk clean.
- **Shoot the hook three ways** (straight, funnier, slower) — 20 extra seconds now, way more options in the edit.
- **Get the customer's OK** before their faces, their boat name, or their group is in frame. Easiest version: shoot it around a real delivery but frame on the cooler, the cart, and you.
- **Don't say the ref code.** The partner page CTA already attributes to `LTYACHTRENTALS`.
- Golden hour looks better but midday matches when deliveries actually happen — midday is fine, just keep the sun behind the camera.
